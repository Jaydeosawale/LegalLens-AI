# LegalLens AI

Short description: Research Indian law and your PDFs with cited AI answers and saved conversations.

Full description:
LegalLens AI helps adults explore legal documents and research questions with AI-assisted answers and source references.

RESEARCH WITH CONTEXT
Ask questions about Indian law and available legal reference material. Answers can include document names and page citations so you can check the underlying sources.

WORK WITH YOUR DOCUMENTS
Upload PDFs, search your document collection, review cited summaries and manage your uploaded files. Document processing and AI requests require an internet connection and are subject to service limits and approval where applicable.

KEEP RESEARCH ORGANIZED
Return to saved conversations, review research history and manage account preferences. Flag an AI answer directly in the app if it is unsafe, incorrect or raises a privacy concern.

KNOW THE LIMITS
LegalLens AI is an independent research tool. It is not a law firm, lawyer, government service or source of guaranteed legal advice. AI output can be incorrect, incomplete or outdated. Always check original official sources and consult a qualified lawyer for your situation. Current legal reference coverage focuses on India, even when the app is accessed from other countries.

DATA AND ACCESS
An email/password account and internet connection are required. Questions and relevant document excerpts are processed by the AI provider. Do not upload confidential, privileged or sensitive personal information. Privacy and account-deletion instructions are available in Account Settings and on the linked website. Free hosted services may take time to wake and have usage limits.
